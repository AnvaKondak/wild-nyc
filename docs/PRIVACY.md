# Wild Neighbors Privacy

**We notice. We don't follow.**

_Updated October 6, 2026_

Wild Neighbors works at the level of a neighborhood, never you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Your location

If you choose to use your location, the app asks your phone once, at low accuracy, and turns your position into a neighborhood cell, an area about 1.2 by 0.6 kilometers, right on your phone. Your exact location is never stored and never leaves your phone. You can also pick a neighborhood from the list instead.

## What leaves your phone

Only two things, both about your neighborhood, not you. To get recent sightings, the app sends the Wild Neighbors server your neighborhood cell, as part of the web address, with no account, device ID or cookies; the server doesn't record your IP address alongside it. To get the weather, the app sends Open-Meteo the center of your neighborhood cell, rounded to about a kilometer. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy covers their service. Our hosting provider may keep standard network logs for security, as all web hosts do.

## What stays on your phone

Your saved neighborhoods, your kindness checkmarks for the season, your sound and morning-note settings, and a cache of recent sightings. Deleting the app deletes all of it. The photos, sounds and stories are built into the app, so looking at them doesn't send anything anywhere.

## The morning note

The daily note is off until you turn it on. It's scheduled on your phone, a week at a time, and no push service or server is involved.

## The animals' privacy, too

Sightings come from iNaturalist and eBird, counted by neighborhood. We never show where an individual animal is, and any location iNaturalist hides to protect a species stays hidden.

## What we don't do

No accounts. No ads. No analytics or tracking. We don't sell or share data, because we don't collect any about you. Wild Neighbors doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new date. Questions? Write to the email on our App Store page.
