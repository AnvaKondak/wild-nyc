## Description (4000)

Meet the neighbors.

Somewhere between your second meeting and your third coffee, you glance out the window and wonder: what are the pigeons doing right now? Now you can find out.

The pigeons on the ledge. The sparrows in the hedge. The squirrel who definitely buried something in your planter. Wild Neighbors is a little app about the everyday animals who share New York City and Jersey City with you, and what they're up to right now.

A NEW STORY EVERY DAY
Each time you open the app, follow one neighbor through their day: who they run into, how the weather feels to them, and what they're doing at this very hour. Mornings, afternoons, evenings and the middle of the night all look (and sound) different, the same way your block does.

YOUR NEIGHBORHOOD, NOT JUST "THE CITY"
Pick from 40 neighborhoods and parks across all five boroughs and Jersey City. Each one has its own cast, based on real sightings: coyotes in Central Park, wild turkeys on Staten Island, bats over Exchange Place, and pigeons pretty much everywhere.

GET TO KNOW EVERYONE
56 neighbors, from pigeons and cardinals to raccoons, red foxes, fireflies and monarchs. Each has a page with their personality, where they live, what they're doing this season, a fun fact, and photos from the iNaturalist community.

PICK YOUR FAVORITES
Heart the neighbors you love most and they get a story of their own every day. Pigeons included, obviously.

BE A GOOD NEIGHBOR
Small kindnesses for every season, like leaving the leaves and turning lights out on big migration nights. And if you ever find a hurt animal, there's a page that tells you what to do right now and who to call.

CALM BY DESIGN
Soft forest birdsong in the background (off until you turn it on). At most one gentle morning note a day, only if you want it. No streaks, no guilt, no endless feed.

WE NOTICE. WE DON'T FOLLOW.
The app never asks for your location: you pick your neighborhood from a list. It's about kinds of animals, never individuals, and it never shows where any animal is. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

## App Review notes

No account or login is needed. On first launch, tap Next through the three welcome pages, pick any neighborhood (try "Central Park"), and optionally pick favorites. The app never requests location; the only network request is the weather for the chosen neighborhood (Open-Meteo). The background sound is off until turned on with the speaker button on the home screen. Animal photos are CC-licensed from iNaturalist and credited on each species page; the birdsong is "Birdsong morning 01" by Matutinho (CC BY-SA 4.0), credited on the Kindness tab.
