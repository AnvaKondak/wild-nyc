The Loyal Homebody
Mates for life
Remembers faces
Loves a ledge

Pairs usually stay together and come back to the same ledge year after year. Both parents feed their chicks a milk they make in their own throats.

Where they came from

Wild rock doves nested on sea cliffs. People tamed them thousands of years ago, carried them everywhere, then let them go. To a pigeon, your building is a cliff.

In their lives right now

Still nesting. City pigeons can raise babies in any season, so some ledges near you have eggs on them even now.

Why you never see baby pigeons
They stay tucked in the nest until they're nearly grown. By the time you see one, it just looks like a slightly scruffy adult.
A small kindness
Look at their feet.
String and hair get wrapped around pigeon toes and slowly cut off circulation. If you spot one, the Wild Bird Fund can help.
