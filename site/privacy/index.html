Wild Neighbors

Privacy, gently

We notice. We don't follow.

Updated October 16, 2026

Wild Neighbors works at the level of a neighborhood, never you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

The app never asks for your location. You pick your neighborhood from a list, and that's all it knows: an area about ten blocks across, never where you are.

What leaves your phone

Only one thing: to get the weather, the app sends Open-Meteo the center of the neighborhood you picked, rounded to about a kilometer. No account, device ID or cookies go with it. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy covers their service.

What stays on your phone

Your saved neighborhoods, your favorites, your kindness checkmarks for the season, your sound and morning-note settings, and a cache of recent sightings. Deleting the app deletes all of it. The photos, sounds and stories are built into the app, so looking at them doesn't send anything anywhere.

The morning note

The daily note is off until you turn it on. It's scheduled on your phone, a week at a time, and no push service or server is involved.

The animals' privacy, too

The app is about species, never individual animals. It never shows where any animal is: it talks about neighborhoods, parks and blocks, not spots.

What we don't do

No accounts. No ads. No analytics or tracking. We don't sell or share data, because we don't collect any about you. Wild Neighbors doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new date. Questions? Write to irisadornnyc@gmail.com.
