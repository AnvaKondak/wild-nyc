Wild Neighbors

Meet the neighbors

Wild Neighbors is a little iOS app about the everyday animals on your block in NYC and Jersey City: what the pigeons, sparrows, squirrels and crows near you are up to right now.

Questions, ideas or a bug?

Write to irisadornnyc@gmail.com. A real person reads every note.

Where does the app work?

In New York City (all five boroughs) and Jersey City. You pick your neighborhood from a list; the app never asks where you are.

How do I change my neighborhood?

Your neighborhoods are the pills at the top of the home screen: tap one to switch, “+” to add another, or the pencil on Home to move it.

How do I pick favorites?

Tap the heart on any neighbor's page, or on the favorites step when you join. Your favorites get their own story every day.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker on the home screen. The sound is calm forest birdsong, and it stays off until you turn it back on.

I found a hurt animal. What do I do?

Open the Kindness tab and tap “Found a hurt animal?” for who to call in NYC and Jersey City. Please don't pick up a wild animal unless a rehabber tells you to.

Can I delete my data?

Everything the app keeps lives only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There's no account to close.
